# Worked examples (dry run, 2026-11-25)

Two finished directed prompts on the cinema-director-v3 spine, written by Cue from this skill. A is live action (direction partly unreachable from the frame: see how the notes flag it). B is anime (2D lock block mapped onto the spine). Copy the structure, never the content.

## A prompt

```
1 continuous shot. Total 4 seconds, no cuts, no transitions. Real-time throughout, no slow motion, no speed change anywhere in this shot.

Style: photorealism, real organic film grain and soft halation, high dynamic range, large-format film look, one dominant hue of cool lavender-violet. NOT a 3D render, NOT a game engine, NOT a game-cutscene aesthetic, NOT a cartoon.
Technical: real-time 24fps, true 180-degree shutter with a real 1/48 second exposure, genuine photographic motion blur, each frame blending smoothly into the next. Smooth stable motion, no flicker, no warping, no morphing, no frame interpolation, no frame blending, no ghosting.
Skin: pore-level realism, visible pores, fine vellus hair, natural asymmetry, no smoothing, no retouching.

NO ON-SCREEN TEXT — CRITICAL: no on-screen text of any kind anywhere in frame at any point. No captions, no subtitles, no titles, no title cards, no credits, no watermarks, no logos, no timecode, no UI overlays, no interface elements. The frame is clean of all overlay graphics from first frame to last.

THE PUSH-IN — CRITICAL: the camera creeps straight forward along the lens axis for the entire 4 seconds at a constant 0.05 m/s, from 1.2 m to 1.0 m from her face, easing in over the first 0.5 s and easing out to a dead stop at 4.0 s. The frame travels from a medium close-up (mid-chest to the crown, the crown at the top edge) to a close-up (collar and dupatta leave the bottom of frame, her chin sits near the lower edge, the top of the crown crops off). The camera height stays at her eye height and the lens axis stays horizontal, 0 degrees of tilt and 0 degrees of roll. The only camera move is this push.

THE EYELINE — CRITICAL: first beat, her eyes rest on the horizon at frame right (screen-relative) and her head stays still. Second beat, on the exhale, only her eyes travel: they slide screen-left about 20 degrees of eye rotation and settle on a point just past the lens, about 40 cm beside the camera body on the camera-left side, passing near the lens without locking onto it. Her head turns no more than 3 degrees. Her mouth stays closed. The mole stays on her near RIGHT cheek.

THE RIM HOLDS — CRITICAL: the thin warm rim on her far contour and temple strands keeps its exact width and brightness for all 4 seconds, under 5% of the frame. The near cheek stays cool lavender. The horizon at frame right stays pale lavender haze: no sun disc, no flare, no warm light spreading.

START FRAME = the approved opening composition: one woman, close-up in 3/4 profile facing frame right, charcoal band-collar kurta, ivory dupatta on her far shoulder, loose temple strands, thin silver stud in her far nostril, small dark mole on the near right cheek, rooftop haze behind. Controls composition, identity, wardrobe, set and light. 100% match to the start frame, exact face, hair, wardrobe and skin tone.

GEOMETRY MAP: she sits left of centre facing frame right, her face filling about 40% of frame height at frame one and about 48% at 4.0 s. Depth planes nearest to furthest: face and near cheek sharp, far shoulder and dupatta slightly soft, parapet ledge soft, city rooftops and horizon haze thrown soft. Her eyeline travels from frame right toward the camera-left side of the lens.

FIRST FRAME: open on the composition of the start frame exactly, already creeping. No empty establishing hold.

LENS LOCK = 29 degrees (85mm) portrait compression, shallow depth of field, focus held on the near eye, strong compression of the background. NOT wide-angle, no distortion, no deep focus. No focal drift and no zoom: the framing change comes from camera travel only.
CAMERA: locked tripod-weight dolly creep, 0 degree cant, zero shake, zero sway, no handheld, perfectly stable, weighty and inevitable, the parallax between her face and the soft rooftops grows slightly as it advances.

LIGHT: unchanged from the start frame. A low sun just below frame right gives one thin warm rim (about 2000K, hard, thin) on her far contour, brow edge and backlit strands only; the near cheek stays in soft cool sky fill (about 9000K); no front fill, no reflector, no beauty light. Held constant for the whole shot.
COLOUR: ~85% cool lavender-violet from sky, haze and the near cheek; ~10% charcoal kurta and ivory dupatta; under 5% orange, only the thin rim.

ATMOSPHERE: naturally hazy exterior, cool violet haze at uniform density, visibility falling with distance, drifting very slowly across the city. Depth planes: face, shoulder, ledge, rooftops, horizon. Never orange or brown. No plumes, no tendrils, no swirls, no fog-machine texture, no volumetric shafts, no god rays.

ACTION TIMING:
0.0-1.8s she holds, eyes on the horizon at frame right, breath barely moving the shoulders; the push has started and the collar begins to slip down out of frame.
1.8-4.0s a slow exhale through the nose, the shoulders settle 3 mm, her eyes slide to the point just past the lens and settle there, one soft blink at about 3.0s; the push eases to a dead stop at 4.0s and the frame holds on the close-up.
Four layers: character motion, exhale and eye travel; micro-motion, one blink and two temple strands stirred about 1 cm by a light breeze that fall back; environmental motion, slow haze drift; camera motion, the straight push only.

PHYSICS: real body weight settling on the exhale, shoulder dropping 3 mm, fine hair strands lagging the breeze and falling back under gravity, the dupatta barely moving, soft contact shadow where the kurta collar meets her neck. Nothing floats, nothing slides.
ACTING: internal, held composure; brow still and relaxed, a faint softening at the brow on the exhale, no smile, eyes alive with a visible catch-light and a slow glide, then a quiet steady look past the lens, never a stare into it.

AUDIO: SFX only — 0.0-4.0s steady soft wind across the rooftop with the faint hiss of fine hair strands, a very low far-off city air bed, 1.8-3.0s one quiet audible exhale close to the mic, 3.0s a soft cloth rustle of the collar at the blink. NO music, NO score, NO BGM — no background music of any kind: no soundtrack, no instrumental, no ambient musical pad, no drone, no tone bed, no swell, no humming, no singing, no lyrics, no voiceover. No voices, no call to prayer, no chanting.

LOCKS: the sequence runs in order — creep begins on the held profile, exhale with eyes travelling past the lens, blink, push stops on the close-up. Same woman, same face and skin, same charcoal band-collar kurta, dupatta on the far shoulder, mole on the near RIGHT cheek, same rooftop haze. Camera stays at eye height, level, on the lens axis, with the 29 degree (85mm) lens throughout. Rim, light and haze constant. Skin reads true cinematic matte, real fine pore texture, real peach fuzz at the hairline, never plastic, never doll-skin.
```

## B prompt

```
1 continuous shot. Total 5 seconds, no cuts, no transitions. Real-time throughout, no speed change anywhere in this shot.

Style: 2D hand-drawn anime, cel-shaded flat colour with one shadow tone on the character, clean thin dark-brown linework exactly as in the start frame, hand-painted detailed background with photographic detail painted by brush. NOT 3D, NOT CGI render, NOT live action, NOT a photograph, NOT semi-real shading, NOT realistic skin. Line weight, palette and character model stay identical to the start frame in every frame, line weight identical in close and wide framing, outlines stable and the same thickness on every frame, colours flat and constant.
Technical: character, hair and skirt animated on twos: each drawing held for two frames (12 drawings a second), stepped pose-to-pose motion, no in-between blending, no motion interpolation, no motion blur on the character. Camera and background layers move smoothly.

NO ON-SCREEN TEXT — CRITICAL: no on-screen text of any kind anywhere in frame at any point. No captions, no subtitles, no titles, no title cards, no credits, no watermarks, no logos, no timecode, no UI overlays, no interface elements. The frame is clean of all overlay graphics from first frame to last.

THE TWO-DIMENSIONAL LOOK — CRITICAL: use the image as the exact first frame and keep its art style unchanged: same line weight, same flat colours, same single shadow tone, same character design. The girl's hair shape and colour, school uniform, proportions and the crossing stay exactly as drawn. Facial features, if any turn into view, keep their exact shapes and spacing; no off-model drawings. Backgrounds are flat painted layers that slide without changing shape.

THE TILT — CRITICAL: one camera move for the whole 5 seconds: a smooth tilt up along the tall painted layers, constant slow speed (about 8 degrees of view per second feel), easing in over 0.5 s and easing out to a stop at 5.0 s. It begins on the start composition with her shoes and the crossing rails at the bottom of frame and travels up her legs, back and hair until her head sits in the lower third and the twilight sky fills the upper two thirds. Layers slide at different speeds: the foreground rail and crossing bar fastest, the character layer next, the mid city and power lines slower, the sky slowest. No zoom, no roll, no shake.

THE TRAIN — CRITICAL: at 2.5 s a train enters from frame left and passes behind her at constant speed, crossing the whole frame by 4.5 s, on the mid layer between her and the city. It is a flat hand-drawn train layer: a row of lit windows, a dark body with the same thin outlines and one shadow tone, passing at a constant horizontal slide, with its window lights streaking as flat bands of warm colour. It stays behind her and never overlaps her body.

START FRAME = the approved composition: a teenage girl in a school uniform standing on a railway crossing at dusk, three-quarter back view, wind in her hair. Controls composition, identity, uniform, set and palette. 100% match to the start frame.

GEOMETRY MAP: she stands right of centre on the crossing, her back three-quarter to the camera, facing screen-left into the distance. Depth planes nearest to furthest: foreground rails and crossing bar (sliding fastest), her layer, the train track and the train lane behind her, the low city and power lines, the sky.

FIRST FRAME: open on the start composition exactly, shoes at the bottom of frame, wind already moving her hair. No empty establishing hold.

LAYOUT AND LENS: wide-angle painted layout, about 24mm feel (74 degree view), low horizon, vast sky; this is a flat artwork tilt, not a physical dolly, no perspective change.
CAMERA: smooth tilt up along the painted layers, parallax of separated layers, as in THE TILT. Locked-off in every other respect.

LIGHT AND COMPOSITING: twilight gradient sky from deep blue at the top through violet and magenta to orange at the horizon, teal shadows on the ground, warm rim light on her hair and shoulder edges from the low sun. Compositing: strong soft bloom on the sky and window lights, one soft cross-shaped lens flare on the sun at the horizon, visible light shafts, floating dust motes, soft depth blur on the foreground rail, light film grain. The flare and bloom stay within the sky and never wash over her linework.
COLOUR: ~60% twilight blue-violet sky and shadow, ~25% orange and magenta horizon glow and rim light, ~15% her uniform and the dark city; each tied to a painted source.

ATMOSPHERE: clear dusk air, thin warm haze low over the city, hand-painted cloud shapes drifting slowly. No fog-machine texture, no realistic volumetric rendering.

ACTION TIMING:
0.0-2.5s she stands still, hand-drawn on twos: only her hair and the pleats of her skirt move in a steady breeze, each hair clump swaying and returning in stepped drawings; the tilt begins; crossing lights in the background blink red.
2.5-5.0s the train passes behind her; its wind arrives as a stronger gust, her hair streams out horizontally and the skirt hem lifts and snaps back, stepped on twos; the tilt eases to a stop with the sky filling the upper two thirds and her head in the lower third; the last train car clears at 4.5s and the hair settles.
Four layers: character motion, minimal (a small shoulder shift from the gust); micro-motion, hair and skirt on twos; environmental motion, drifting clouds, dust motes, blinking crossing lights, the train; camera motion, the tilt only.
No smears, no impact frames, no speed lines.

PHYSICS: drawn follow-through, hair and skirt hem lagging the gust with overlapping action, a soft settle after the train passes, simple shadow shape on the ground under her shoes.
ACTING: still posture, her back to us, weight on both feet, her head unmoving; the gust only moves her hair.

AUDIO: SFX only — 0.0-2.5s steady dusk wind, distant cicadas, the soft clack of crossing lights; 2.2s level-crossing warning bell begins and rings through the pass; 2.5-4.5s train rumble and rail clatter rising and falling as it passes, a short pitched whoosh of wind at the pass; 4.5-5.0s bell fades, wind returns. NO music, NO score, NO BGM, NO opening or insert song, NO voiceover, no dialogue.

Same girl, same uniform, same hair colour and shape, same crossing, same line weight and single shadow tone in every frame. Character on twos, camera smooth. Palette and light direction constant.
```
