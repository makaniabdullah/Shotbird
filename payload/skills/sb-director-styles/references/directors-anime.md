# Anime film direction: craft reference for image-to-video prompting (Seedance 2.5)

Purpose: give a prompt writer enough concrete craft language to write image-to-video prompts for anime films, with a start frame as reference.

How to read it:
- Part A entries describe a look. The "Prompt translation" lines are the only part meant to be pasted, and they never name a person, studio or title.
- "Lens" values are implied focal lengths read from the layouts. Animation has no physical lens, so treat them as descriptions of perspective, not facts about equipment.
- Sound fields list sound effects only. No music, no songs, anywhere.
- "(unverified)" marks a specific claim that was not confirmed against a source in this research pass. Unmarked stylistic readings are standard critical observation, cross-checked where a source is listed at the end.

---

# Part A. Directors and action styles

## Hayao Miyazaki
- **Signature:** Hand-drawn full-bodied motion in lush painted worlds, with quiet pauses between bursts of flight and running.
- **Layout and lens:** Normal to mild wide perspective (about 35–50mm feel). Eye-level or child-height camera. Deep, readable staging with clear ground planes, big skies, wide establishing shots, then medium shots that keep the whole body in frame.
- **Camera:** Mostly locked layouts with slow lateral pans and tilts over tall or wide background paintings. Follow shots on running and flying, with cloud and ground layers sliding in parallax. Very little zoom. Flight scenes use background animation (the ground itself is redrawn).
- **Timing:** Mixed ones and twos, generous drawing counts. Strong anticipation, weight shift, overshoot and settle. Bodies have mass: a character braces before lifting. Few smears, no impact frames.
- **Acting:** Everyday business done properly: tying shoes, cutting bread, climbing stairs on all fours. Hair bristles or lifts when emotion surges (unverified as a stated rule, but widely observed). Wide mouths when shouting, big rolling tears, cloth that inflates in wind.
- **Backgrounds:** Hand-painted poster-colour scenery by background painters such as Kazuo Oga: layered foliage, cumulus clouds, weathered European or rural Japanese buildings. Painterly, not photographic. Character lines are thin and dark brown to black, uniform weight.
- **Light and compositing:** Restrained. Flat cel characters with one shadow tone over a painted plate. Dappled leaf shadow, soft sky glow. No lens flare, no depth blur, almost no bloom.
- **Colour:** Saturated greens, sky blue, cream cloud whites, terracotta roofs, warm skin. Night scenes in deep blue-green.
- **FX:** Hand-drawn wind in grass, billowing cloud, blobby viscous liquids, rounded smoke puffs, sea swells drawn as solid shapes.
- **Sound effects:** Wind through grass and trees, propeller and engine drone, creaking wood, bare feet on floorboards, cicadas, rain on a roof, long near-silent pauses (the director has described these pauses as "ma", per his 2002 Ebert interview).
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "hand-drawn 2D cel animation over a hand-painted gouache background"
  - "thin uniform dark-brown outlines, flat colour with a single soft shadow tone"
  - "wind moves the grass, hair and loose clothing in overlapping waves"
  - "weighty full-body movement with clear anticipation and settle"
  - "towering white cumulus clouds, saturated summer greens"
  - "the character pauses for a breath and simply looks, nothing else moves but the wind"
- **Avoid:** Lens flares and bloom. Photoreal backgrounds. Impact frames and speed lines. Glossy gradient shading.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for flight, nature, children, food, gentle wonder. Worst for neon, gore, stylised sakuga fights.

## Isao Takahata
- **Signature:** Observational realism that later dissolves into sketch and watercolour, with the paper left showing.
- **Layout and lens:** Eye-level, normal lens feel (about 50mm), modest and theatre-like. Subjects sit in the middle distance. In the late style the frame edges fade to white paper and backgrounds are left unfinished on purpose.
- **Camera:** Mostly static. Slow, short pans. The camera observes and rarely chases. No showy moves.
- **Timing:** Naturalistic everyday acting on twos, careful spacing, long holds. The exception is expressive bursts where line quality itself breaks down: in the late feature's running sequence, animated by Shinji Hashimoto, the lines stretch into a rough whirl of brushstrokes.
- **Acting:** Small truthful gestures: peeling fruit, a child's wobbling crawl, cheek creases when smiling (a deliberate choice in the early-90s rural drama, unverified in this pass). No anime shorthand, no sweat drops.
- **Backgrounds:** Early: detailed, realistic painted settings. Late: pale transparent watercolour by Kazuo Oga that shares one surface with the characters. Character lines are rough pencil or charcoal-like, with visible variation in pressure and gaps where the fill does not reach the line (Osamu Tanabe's roughs were traced with little clean-up, per BFI).
- **Light and compositing:** Almost none. Flat, paper-white light. No bloom, no glow, no blur.
- **Colour:** Pale washes: soft pinks, bamboo green, straw yellow, grey ink. Large areas of white.
- **FX:** Water, petals and wind drawn as loose brush marks. Fire as rough charcoal scribble with red wash.
- **Sound effects:** Insects, birdsong, bamboo creak, cloth rustle, footsteps on tatami and earth, a baby's breath and babble, distant village sounds. Very dry and close.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "loose charcoal pencil outlines with uneven pressure, lines left sketchy and open"
  - "pale transparent watercolour washes that do not fill to the outline"
  - "white paper showing at the edges of the frame, background fading into blank paper"
  - "quiet naturalistic movement, small everyday gestures, long still holds"
  - "no shading on the figures, flat wash colour only"
- **Avoid:** Clean vector lines. Saturated colour. Any compositing glow. Fast cutting.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for folklore, memory, childhood, grief. Worst for action spectacle, technology, night neon.

## Satoshi Kon
- **Signature:** Realistically drawn people in a believable city, joined by match cuts that slide between reality, memory and performance.
- **Layout and lens:** Live-action framing with a normal to mild wide lens (about 28–50mm feel), eye-level, accurate perspective and correct human proportions. Mirrors, windows, screens and posters are used as frames within the frame. Cramped apartments, train carriages, back streets.
- **Camera:** Mostly static, well-composed shots. Motion comes from the edit: match cuts on action, graphic matches, a character walking out of one scene into another. Occasional follow shot on a run with full background animation.
- **Timing:** Realistic acting on twos with solid volumes. Few smears. Running is weighty and slightly ungainly. Abrupt freezes and sudden starts serve the cut.
- **Acting:** Faces with real bone structure, lines and asymmetry. Forced public smiles that drop. Eye darts, swallowed breath, glances at reflections. Hair and cloth behave plainly.
- **Backgrounds:** Dense, realistic, lived-in clutter: wiring, signage, laundry, stacked magazines. Painted but accurate. Clean, thin, dark outlines of even weight.
- **Light and compositing:** Naturalistic. Fluorescent interiors, grey overcast daylight, stage spotlights. Little bloom. Glows reserved for screens and stage lights.
- **Colour:** Desaturated urban neutrals with one strong accent, often red. Parade or dream scenes jump to dense, saturated toy colours.
- **FX:** Minimal. Rain, breath, snow, glass. Dream scenes use morphing objects and swarming crowds.
- **Sound effects:** Camera shutters, phone and fax tones, train rumble, crowd murmur, apartment hum, a door chain, footsteps in a stairwell, sudden dead silence.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "realistically proportioned 2D anime characters with detailed, individual adult faces"
  - "cluttered realistic urban interior, accurate perspective, thin clean outlines"
  - "muted grey-beige palette with a single saturated red accent"
  - "the character's reflection in the window moves a fraction out of sync"
  - "locked-off camera, natural restrained acting, a sudden head turn at the end"
  - "ends on a clean, centred pose suitable for a match cut"
- **Avoid:** Big-eyed cute designs. Speed lines. Heavy glow. Floaty camera moves.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for psychological drama, identity, city life, dream logic. Worst for mecha, pastoral fantasy, slapstick.

## Makoto Shinkai
- **Signature:** Photo-accurate skies and cities drenched in light, with simply drawn figures inside them.
- **Layout and lens:** Wide-angle scenery (about 18–28mm feel) with exaggerated sky area and low horizons. Long-lens inserts of everyday objects: phones, rails, signals, vending machines, shoes at a crossing. Figures are often small, back to camera, facing the sky.
- **Camera:** Slow pans and tilts up to the sky. Gentle push-ins. Digital multiplane with many layers. Time-lapse clouds and light sweeping across a city. Rack focus between foreground and background layers.
- **Timing:** Character animation is modest, on twos and threes, with long holds. The motion budget goes to environment: clouds, rain, trains, light. Short insert shots cut quickly in montage.
- **Acting:** Restrained. A held gaze, a hand tightening on a strap, a head turning too late. Hair lifted by a passing train's wind.
- **Backgrounds:** Detailed digital paintings worked up over photographic reference, more detailed than the characters. Reflections on wet tarmac, glass and rails. Character linework is thin; shadow shapes on characters often carry a warm or coloured rim from the light source (unverified as a formal rule).
- **Light and compositing:** The defining layer. Lens flares (which he calls a signature going back to his self-produced shorts, per VFX Voice), streaking sunbeams, strong bloom on highlights, rim-lit hair, floating dust and rain particles, depth blur, glints on water drops.
- **Colour:** Twilight gradients: magenta into orange into deep blue. Teal shadows. Emerald summer greens. Night scenes in blue with warm window light.
- **FX:** Rain in sheets with individual splashes, snow, falling petals, comet or meteor trails, fast-moving volumetric cloud.
- **Sound effects:** Level-crossing bells, train passing, cicadas, rain on an umbrella, phone vibration, distant traffic, wind on a rooftop, footsteps on wet pavement.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "2D anime characters over a highly detailed, photo-accurate painted city background"
  - "low horizon, vast sky filling two thirds of the frame, towering backlit clouds"
  - "strong sun flare, bloom on highlights, visible light shafts, floating dust motes"
  - "twilight gradient sky from orange to deep blue, teal shadows, warm rim light on hair"
  - "wet reflective ground, raindrops catching the light"
  - "the clouds drift and the light slowly shifts while the figure stays almost still"
- **Avoid:** Flat, compositing-free looks. Heavy character acting. Thick outlines. Muddy palettes.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for romance, longing, weather, city and sky beauty shots. Worst for comedy, dense fight choreography.

## Mamoru Hosoda
- **Signature:** Clean, shadowless characters with lively full-body acting, set against warm painted family life or a white digital world.
- **Layout and lens:** Flat, frontal, side-on staging with a normal lens feel. Wide shots that hold a whole room or house front. The same layout is revisited across time to show change (unverified in this pass, widely noted by critics). Characters are often small in frame.
- **Camera:** Long lateral tracking shots that scroll past rooms or corridors. Locked wide shots held while people enter and leave. Digital-world scenes use free 3D camera rotation.
- **Timing:** Full-body acting on twos with strong silhouettes. Elastic, bouncy running and falling. Children move in bursts. Holds are short.
- **Acting:** Whole-body emotion: slumps, stomps, flailing arms, wide-open crying mouths, red flushed faces. Simple dot or line eyes at distance.
- **Backgrounds:** Detailed, warm, painted real-world settings (summer houses, city crossings). Characters are drawn "kagenashi": flat colour, no shadow tone and no highlights, which Sakuga Blog reports he treats as a core principle. Digital worlds are white voids with red-outlined figures (unverified here; visible in the 2009 feature).
- **Light and compositing:** Light and clean. Bright, even daylight. Soft sky glow. No heavy bloom or grain.
- **Colour:** Summer blue sky, white cumulus, fresh green, clean skin tones. Digital world: white, pastel, red line.
- **FX:** Towering summer clouds, rain, snow drifts, wind gusts. Digital-world swarms of small 3DCG objects.
- **Sound effects:** Cicadas, electric fan, wind chime, floorboards, family chatter as walla, cutlery, rain, keyboard clatter, a train crossing.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "flat-colour 2D anime characters with no shadows and no highlights, clean thin outlines"
  - "side-on wide shot, the camera tracks sideways at a steady speed, layers sliding in parallax"
  - "lively full-body acting, bouncy energetic movement, clear silhouettes"
  - "warm detailed painted summer background, bright even daylight, tall white clouds"
  - "the camera holds wide while the character runs in and out of frame"
- **Avoid:** Shadowed, glossy character shading. Tight face close-ups throughout. Dark, gritty palettes.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for family stories, summer, children and animals, virtual-world sequences. Worst for noir, horror, heavy-effects battles.

## Katsuhiro Otomo
- **Signature:** Dense, mechanically exact cityscapes and destruction drawn with full animation and hard perspective.
- **Layout and lens:** Deep-focus wide-angle perspective (about 20–28mm feel) with strict vanishing points. High and low angles on huge structures. Crowds and vehicles fill the frame. Small, realistically proportioned figures against megastructures.
- **Camera:** Fast tracking with full background animation. Vehicles followed at speed. Slow reveals up vast buildings. Elaborate multi-layer camera stand moves.
- **Timing:** Large sections of the 1988 feature were shot on ones (24 drawings a second), unusual for Japanese animation of the time, across roughly 160,000 cels. Heavy, realistic weight. Dialogue was pre-recorded so mouths were animated to match the words.
- **Acting:** Realistic bodies, small eyes, natural proportions. Sweat, grimaces, bared teeth. Mouth shapes follow speech closely instead of simple flaps.
- **Backgrounds:** Extremely detailed painted cityscapes, mostly at night: lit windows, pipes, cables, rubble. Fine, even, black technical linework. Machines drawn with engineering logic.
- **Light and compositing:** Optical cel-era glows: light trails from tail-lights, backlit window light, lens flare from headlamps, film grain, slight halation.
- **Colour:** The feature is reported to have used 327 colours, 50 mixed specially, largely because it is set at night: reds, oranges, teal-greens, deep blues.
- **FX:** Hand-drawn smoke that rolls in rounded volumes, collapsing concrete with individual fragments, shock domes, glass bursts, water surges, organic body mutation.
- **Sound effects:** Motorbike engines and tyre squeal, heavy machinery, sirens, crowd roar, glass and concrete collapse, electrical crackle, a low sub rumble before a blast.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "1980s theatrical cel animation, fully animated with fluid, weighty movement"
  - "vast night cityscape in strict deep perspective, thousands of lit windows, fine technical linework"
  - "red tail-lights leave long glowing light trails"
  - "hand-drawn rolling smoke and crumbling concrete with individual debris pieces"
  - "realistically proportioned characters, small eyes, film grain and slight halation"
- **Avoid:** Soft pastel palettes. Cute proportions. Limited, held-pose animation. Clean digital gradients.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for cyberpunk, vehicles, mass destruction, crowds. Worst for gentle slice-of-life, minimal designs.

## Mamoru Oshii
- **Signature:** Still, contemplative realism: empty cities, reflections, and long silences between short, precise bursts of action.
- **Layout and lens:** Layout-led filmmaking (he called his 1993 feature a "layout film", built from location scouting and photos). Telephoto compression for cityscapes, and fisheye or extreme wide distortion for unease, which he chose for the anxiety it creates. Faces framed frontally and expressionless. Reflections in glass, water and visors.
- **Camera:** Long static holds. Very slow pans across a city with no characters. Slow push-ins on faces. Montages of urban stills. Action shots are brief and clear.
- **Timing:** Limited movement, long held cels, tiny motions (a blink, a ripple). Action is realistic and weighty, on twos, with accurate recoil.
- **Acting:** Minimal. Flat faces, little blinking, eyes that stare. Stillness reads as thought. Hair and cloth barely move.
- **Backgrounds:** Highly detailed, realistic, photo-referenced cityscapes: canals, signage, concrete, haze. Thin restrained linework. Birds, a basset hound and aircraft recur (the birds are deliberate "noise" with no meaning, per his production notes; the hound is unverified in this pass).
- **Light and compositing:** Hazy, overcast, low contrast. Monitor glow on faces. Rain on glass. Soft diffusion, light film grain. Later work adds heavy digital filters in amber.
- **Colour:** Grey-green, teal and slate, with amber or sodium-orange in later work. Desaturated skin.
- **FX:** Rain, water ripples, drifting snow, thermoptic shimmer, muzzle flash with shell casings, flocks of birds.
- **Sound effects:** Rain, distant city hum, helicopter rotors, water lapping, footsteps echoing in empty concrete, radio chatter, single clean gunshots, long silence.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "realistic 2D anime, detailed overcast cityscape, hazy low-contrast light"
  - "slight fisheye distortion at the frame edges" or "long-lens compressed view of stacked buildings"
  - "the frame is almost completely still; only rain and a slow water ripple move"
  - "expressionless face lit by cold monitor glow, reflection in the glass"
  - "muted grey-green and teal palette, soft diffusion, fine film grain"
- **Avoid:** Lively acting. Bright primaries. Fast cutting. Comic exaggeration.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for sci-fi mood, political thriller, philosophy, atmosphere. Worst for comedy, romance warmth, high-energy sakuga.

## Hideaki Anno
- **Signature:** Graphic, rigid layouts and very long holds that explode into dense mechanical and explosion animation.
- **Layout and lens:** Extreme choices: very long telephoto flattening, or very low wide angles under giant machines. Utility poles, power lines, signals and concrete are treated as subjects (he wanted poles and buildings handled as characters, per Kazuya Tsurumaki). Tilted horizons, figures small in a corner, strong silhouettes against sunset, frame-filling close-ups of eyes and hands. Live-action lens study is evident in his work (unverified as a stated method).
- **Camera:** Locked-off shots held far longer than expected, sometimes with only a mouth or nothing moving. Hard cuts, flash cuts, text cards. Action uses sudden fast pans and low tracking.
- **Timing:** Radical limited animation in drama (single held cels), then dense full animation for action. Explosions and debris are timed with sharp pops and long lingering smoke.
- **Acting:** Withheld. Averted faces, backs to camera, glasses that hide the eyes with flat glare, hunched shoulders. Emotion arrives as a sudden scream after stillness.
- **Backgrounds:** Realistic infrastructure: rail lines, substations, apartment blocks, control rooms. Clean thin lines. Graphic flat-colour skies.
- **Light and compositing:** Hard top light with black shadow shapes, strong backlight silhouettes, red emergency light, cross-shaped light bursts. Later work adds lens flare and digital gradients.
- **Colour:** Orange sunset against black, emergency red, clinical white and blue, purple-green-orange machines.
- **FX:** Layered explosions, cross flares, shattering geometric barriers, tracer fire, liquid bursts, dust plumes around giant feet.
- **Sound effects:** Loud cicadas, level-crossing bells, train rattle, wires humming, alarms, hydraulic clunks, heavy power-up hum, total silence before impact.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "rigid locked-off shot held completely still, nothing moves except one small element"
  - "utility poles and power lines in black silhouette against a flat orange sunset"
  - "tilted horizon, the figure small in the lower corner of the frame"
  - "extreme telephoto flattening, heat haze, hard top light with solid black shadows"
  - "giant machine seen from a very low wide angle, dust rising around it"
  - "eyes hidden by flat white glare on the glasses"
- **Avoid:** Constant camera drift. Soft pastel light. Busy acting during drama beats. Rounded cute shapes.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for dread, mecha, isolation, institutional spaces. Worst for cosy scenes, flowing romance.

## Shinichirō Watanabe
- **Signature:** Cool, film-literate genre staging: loose, rhythmic action and lived-in worlds, cut like live action.
- **Layout and lens:** Cinematic widescreen thinking with a mix of wide masters and tight inserts (about 24–85mm feel). Low angles on fights, silhouettes in doorways, smoky interiors. Characters lounge in asymmetric compositions. (Layout readings here are critical observation, unverified in this pass.)
- **Camera:** Handheld-feeling follow shots in fights, whip pans, quick punch-ins, spacecraft tracking shots with parallax star fields. Calm locked shots in between.
- **Timing:** Action is choreographed like martial-arts film: clear beats, fluid full-body motion on ones and twos, with weight shifts and counters. Yutaka Nakamura's fight cuts appear in his best-known series and its film. Casual scenes are relaxed and limited.
- **Acting:** Slouches, shrugs, cigarette business, half-lidded eyes, loose-limbed walks. Long lanky proportions. Coats and ties trail with follow-through.
- **Backgrounds:** Detailed, grimy, multicultural cities and ship interiors. Painted with texture: rust, stains, neon signage. Medium-weight dark outlines.
- **Light and compositing:** Noir lighting: hard side light, venetian-blind shadows, backlit smoke, neon spill. Cel-era grain in the older work.
- **Colour:** Muted earth tones and navy with warm yellow or red accents. Sepia flashbacks.
- **FX:** Cigarette smoke, muzzle flash, sparks, splashing water, spacecraft exhaust trails, dust in light beams.
- **Sound effects:** Footsteps on metal, lighter click, gunshots with room echo, ship engine hum, rain, bar ambience as walla, cloth whoosh in kicks, shell casings on the floor.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "1990s cel-animated look, lanky realistically drawn adults, medium dark outlines, film grain"
  - "fluid martial-arts choreography with clear weight shifts, the camera follows loosely as if handheld"
  - "smoky interior, hard side light through blinds, neon spill from the window"
  - "muted navy and earth palette with one warm yellow accent"
  - "relaxed slouching posture, coat tails trailing with follow-through"
- **Avoid:** Stiff symmetric framing. Candy colours. Over-glowing digital effects. Chibi gags.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for noir, space western, street-level action, cool mood pieces. Worst for earnest pastoral fantasy.

## Masaaki Yuasa
- **Signature:** Elastic, rubbery drawing where perspective, proportion and colour bend freely with emotion.
- **Layout and lens:** Wildly distorted wide-angle (fisheye-like) perspective that swings during a shot. Flat graphic compositions next to deep warped ones. Camera height anywhere, including directly above or below.
- **Camera:** The drawing is the camera: backgrounds are redrawn so space rotates and stretches. Fast swooping moves through morphing space. Split screens and graphic inserts.
- **Timing:** Loose and unpredictable. Smooth vector-tweened movement in some work (his studio is known for combining hand-drawn animation with Flash-based digital animation) next to rough hand-drawn bursts. Huge squash and stretch. Limbs lengthen in runs and dances.
- **Acting:** Bodies deform to express feeling: noodle limbs, giant open mouths, simple dot eyes. Dance and running as pure rhythm. Rough, off-model drawing kept on purpose.
- **Backgrounds:** Flat, simplified, often abstract. Solid colour fields, photo-collage or sketchy line. Line weight is thin and wobbly, sometimes coloured.
- **Light and compositing:** Minimal. Flat fills, occasional hard graphic shadow. No realistic bloom or depth blur.
- **Colour:** Bold, unnatural flats: lemon yellow, turquoise, hot pink, red against black. Palette changes per scene with mood.
- **FX:** Water as solid geometric blocks or flat shapes. Abstract morphing transitions. Smoke and fire as simple graphic forms.
- **Sound effects:** Exaggerated, close foley: slapping feet, breath, splashes, squeaks, ball hits, cloth snaps, sudden quiet.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "loose, sketchy 2D animation with thin wobbly outlines and flat unshaded colour"
  - "extreme rubbery squash and stretch, limbs elongate as the character runs"
  - "the perspective warps and swings like a fisheye as the character moves"
  - "bold flat colour fields, a simplified graphic background"
  - "water drawn as flat geometric shapes"
- **Avoid:** On-model polish. Photoreal backgrounds. Glossy compositing. Stiff realistic proportions.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for surreal, comic, musical, sport and dream sequences. Worst for sober realism, detailed mecha.

## Naoko Yamada
- **Signature:** Intimate, camera-conscious observation of small gestures, shot as if through a long lens with soft focus.
- **Layout and lens:** Long-lens compression (about 85–200mm feel) and shallow depth of field. Inserts of legs, feet, hands and the back of the head in place of faces: she has said nervous people move their legs. Off-centre framing with lots of headroom or empty space. Low camera height. Foreground objects blurred across the frame.
- **Camera:** Mostly static with a gentle handheld float. Rack focus between layers. Slow lateral slides. Occasional soft vignette, as if seen through an old lens. She describes herself as working like a cameraman.
- **Timing:** Subtle character acting on twos and threes, with drawings spent on tiny motions: toes curling, fingers pulling a sleeve, a heel lifting. Soft ease in and out. No smears.
- **Acting:** Body language over dialogue. Fidgeting feet, hair touched or tucked, weight shifting, glances that slide away. Hair strands and skirt hems move softly and separately.
- **Backgrounds:** Clean, lightly detailed real-world schools, rivers and stairwells in soft painted finish. Very thin linework, often in a coloured tone instead of black (unverified in this pass).
- **Light and compositing:** Soft diffused window light, strong depth blur, chromatic aberration at the edges, light leaks, bokeh, gentle bloom, pale haze.
- **Colour:** Pale pastels: watery blue, soft pink, cream, pale green. Low contrast, lifted blacks.
- **FX:** Water ripples, petals, floating dust, light flicker through leaves. Occasional abstract colour or ink-in-water inserts.
- **Sound effects:** Indoor shoes on a corridor floor, cloth rustle, breath, a chair scrape, distant school yard, water lapping, a bicycle chain, footsteps on a bridge, close quiet room tone.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "close shot of only the legs and feet, shot as if on a long lens with a very shallow depth of field"
  - "soft blurred foreground object crossing the frame, bokeh highlights"
  - "delicate, thin coloured outlines, pale pastel palette, lifted blacks"
  - "small nervous gestures: toes shift, fingers tighten on the sleeve"
  - "soft diffused window light, slight chromatic aberration at the frame edges"
  - "almost static camera with a faint handheld float, a slow rack focus"
- **Avoid:** Deep-focus wide shots. Hard black lines. Broad acting. Speed lines or impact frames.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for adolescence, friendship, music-room and school scenes, quiet emotion. Worst for battles, large-scale spectacle.

## Kunihiko Ikuhara
- **Signature:** Theatrical, symbolic staging: flat stage-like sets, repeated ritual sequences and graphic emblems.
- **Layout and lens:** Frontal, symmetric, proscenium framing with flat perspective. Characters posed like actors on a stage; critics describe a stage-play presentation where key details are highlighted and secondary ones kept minimal. Frames and borders (roses, bars, windows) around figures. Background crowds reduced to pictogram or silhouette figures (unverified in this pass, visible in his 2011 series).
- **Camera:** Locked symmetric shots, hard cuts, repeated identical sequences reused as ritual (stock or "bank" footage, unverified in this pass). Spinning backgrounds, iris wipes, slow push-ins on emblems.
- **Timing:** Limited animation with strong held poses and maximum effect from few drawings, a debt to Osamu Dezaki. Sword or transformation scenes are stylised, pose to pose.
- **Acting:** Formal, posed, declamatory. Hair and capes sweep in big arcs. Sharp glances, hand-to-chest gestures. Comic relief drops into simplified faces.
- **Backgrounds:** Stylised architecture: endless staircases, arenas, graphic interiors. Flat pattern, hard shapes, silhouette cut-outs. Elegant thin lines with long, sharp character designs.
- **Light and compositing:** Graphic. Spotlights, flat colour washes, hard silhouette shadows, sparkles and petals over the frame.
- **Colour:** Rose pink, red, white, black, royal blue, gold. Strong flat contrasts.
- **FX:** Swirling rose petals, falling feathers, stylised sparkles, spinning emblems, flat graphic slashes.
- **Sound effects:** Bell tolls, sword ring, footsteps climbing stone stairs, a gate mechanism, cloth sweep, train and station announcements, sudden silence.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "symmetrical frontal composition like a theatre stage, flat perspective"
  - "elegant long-limbed 2D anime character holding a formal pose under a single spotlight"
  - "background crowd shown as flat featureless silhouette figures"
  - "rose petals swirl across the frame, hard graphic shadows"
  - "flat rose-pink, red, white and black palette"
  - "limited animation: the pose holds, only the cape and hair sweep"
- **Avoid:** Naturalistic handheld camera. Photoreal backgrounds. Muted realistic palettes.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for allegory, ritual duels, transformation, fashion-like stylisation. Worst for grounded realism, physical action.

## Tetsurō Araki
- **Signature:** Maximal, operatic action: swooping cameras, heavily rendered faces and dramatic shadow.
- **Layout and lens:** Extreme low and high angles, wide-angle foreshortening, dutch tilts. Tight close-ups of eyes and clenched teeth. Tiny figures against giant threats for scale.
- **Camera:** Fast flying follow shots through 3D or 3D-assisted environments, with the camera swinging around characters in flight (the 3D-background method is unverified in this pass). Crash zooms, whip pans, dramatic slow-motion holds.
- **Timing:** Big key poses with long anticipation, fast bursts on ones, then held slow motion. Smears and speed lines in peaks. Impact holds with shake.
- **Acting:** Heightened: screaming, trembling, sweat, bulging eyes. Hair and capes whip in constant wind.
- **Backgrounds:** Detailed, painted, dramatic skies and architecture, textured stone and timber. Thick, bold outlines around characters with heavy black shadow shapes.
- **Light and compositing:** Heavy. Strong rim light, gradient shading on faces and hair, lens flare, dust and sparks. His studio promoted a "makeup animation" pass in his 2016 series, where digital artists add detailed painted finishing to selected close-ups (Sakuga Blog).
- **Colour:** Earthy browns and greens with blood red; or dark steam-punk amber and teal. High contrast.
- **FX:** Steam, dust, flying rubble, blood sprays, sparks, smoke trails from cables or weapons, 3DCG crowds or creatures mixed with drawn ones.
- **Sound effects:** Gas hiss, cable zip, blade ring, heavy footfalls that shake the ground, crumbling masonry, steam locomotive, wind rush, crowd screams.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "bold thick outlines, heavy black shadow shapes, strong rim light"
  - "the camera swoops and banks behind the character through deep parallax layers"
  - "extreme low angle, the giant figure looms over tiny foreground characters"
  - "detailed painted rendering on the face in close-up, gradient-shaded hair, glinting eyes"
  - "dust, sparks and debris fill the air, capes and hair whipping in strong wind"
  - "a held slow-motion pose, then a sudden burst of fast movement"
- **Avoid:** Flat even light. Static talky staging. Pastel softness. Thin delicate lines.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for horror-action, aerial chases, monsters, last stands. Worst for subtle domestic drama.

## Hiroyuki Imaishi
- **Signature:** Hyper-exaggerated, angular, loud cartoon action built from extreme poses and deliberately limited animation.
- **Layout and lens:** Forced perspective with fists, feet and weapons thrust at the lens. Low angles. Graphic compositions with bold on-screen shapes. Jagged, geometric character silhouettes.
- **Camera:** Snap zooms, whip pans, spinning backgrounds, constant background animation with radial speed lines. Shots are short.
- **Timing:** Pose to pose with almost no in-betweens: transitions between poses are cut out and fast loops are used for comedy. A leading figure of the neo-Kanada school. Rapid pops between extremes, heavy smears, long cheeky holds, then bursts. Drama comes from spacing, not drawing count.
- **Acting:** Shouting with huge mouths, jagged teeth, pointed fingers, cross-armed hero poses. Off-model on purpose. Hair and scarves as rigid, spiky shapes.
- **Backgrounds:** Simplified, graphic, flat colour or abstract pattern. Thick, rough, variable outlines with hard black fills.
- **Light and compositing:** Flat and graphic. Solid black shadows, geometric light flares, coloured silhouettes, hard white highlights. Little realistic glow.
- **Colour:** Primary red, yellow and cyan against black. Sudden palette swaps and inverted-colour flashes.
- **FX:** Geometric explosions, drill and spiral trails, angular smoke, star-shaped glints, thick radial speed lines, chunky debris.
- **Sound effects:** Oversized punches, metallic clangs, whooshes, tyre screech, explosions with sharp crack, comic boings and slide whistles used sparingly.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "extreme forced perspective, the fist thrust huge toward the camera"
  - "angular, jagged poses snapping from one extreme to the next with almost no in-between drawings"
  - "thick rough black outlines, flat primary colours, solid black shadow shapes"
  - "geometric star-shaped light flares and radial speed lines in the background"
  - "limited animation with hard holds, then a burst of smeared movement"
- **Avoid:** Realistic weight and proportions. Soft gradients. Slow elegant camera moves. Photoreal backgrounds.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for hype, comedy-action, robots, transformation, finales. Worst for quiet realism, delicate romance.

## Yutaka Nakamura (action-animation style)
- **Signature:** Weighty, choreographed hand-to-hand action where the camera moves with the fight, punctuated by impact frames and cubic debris.
- **Layout and lens:** Wide-angle, close to the fighters, at ground or waist height. Space is clear enough to follow each exchange. Background is often drawn by the animator so the room rotates with the action.
- **Camera:** Fully animated camera: orbiting, tracking and rotating around fighters with redrawn backgrounds. Recognised traits include camera shake on hits, blur, and the bending or stretching of fast-moving objects.
- **Timing:** Mixed frame rates in one shot: threes and fours for anticipation and heavy holds, ones for the strike (unverified in this pass, widely analysed). A one- or two-frame impact frame, then a hit-stop hold, then debris and settle. Long stretched smears on limbs and blades.
- **Acting:** Fighters show stance, guard and recovery. Feet plant and slide. Cloth and hair trail the motion and catch up after.
- **Backgrounds:** Whatever the show uses, but floors and walls crack into simple geometric chunks. His cubic rubble is known among fans as "Yutapon cubes", after his nickname.
- **Light and compositing:** Impact frames: black-and-white or inverted, high-contrast, roughly drawn frames. Colour flashes, radial light, dust haze.
- **Colour:** Show palette, interrupted by monochrome or two-colour flashes at impact.
- **FX:** Cube and slab debris flung outward, ring-shaped shockwaves, dust rings, sparks, blocky water bursts, ground craters.
- **Sound effects:** Sharp crack on impact, a beat of silence on the impact frame, low boom, stone chunks scattering, cloth snap, feet sliding on grit.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "the camera circles the two fighters as they exchange blows, the background rotating with it"
  - "slow heavy wind-up, then the strike lands in an instant"
  - "on impact, one or two frames flash to rough black-and-white high-contrast drawing, then hold"
  - "the floor shatters into simple cube-shaped chunks that fly outward"
  - "limbs and blade stretch into long smears at top speed"
  - "a ring-shaped shockwave and dust burst spread along the ground"
- **Avoid:** Evenly paced motion. Realistic small debris. Constant shake with no stillness. Cuts on every hit.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for one-on-one fights, superpower clashes, sword duels. Worst for dialogue, crowd scenes, gentle material.

## Ufotable (digital compositing studio style)
- **Signature:** 2D characters fused into 3D-built environments by an in-house photography team, with dense glow, particles and stylised elemental effects.
- **Layout and lens:** Dynamic wide-angle action layouts in real 3D space, plus dramatic low angles. Still scenes use cinematic framing with depth blur.
- **Camera:** True 3D camera moves through modelled backgrounds: fast push-throughs, orbits, vertical drops. Hand-drawn characters are matched to the camera path. Drama scenes use slow push-ins.
- **Timing:** Character animation on twos and ones, with crisp key poses, while camera and effects move on ones. Slow-motion beats before finishing blows.
- **Acting:** Standard modern anime acting with detailed eyes. Hair, haori and cloth get strong secondary motion in action.
- **Backgrounds:** Detailed painted textures on 3D geometry, lit to match characters. Character outlines vary in thickness, with thicker contour lines than interior lines in the best-known recent work (unverified in this pass).
- **Light and compositing:** The defining feature. The director of photography, Yuichi Terao, describes the job as merging characters, backgrounds and effects into one image with final say on lighting and colour; with correct lighting, characters blend into any background. Layered gradient shadows for depth, a dark area kept even in bright scenes, bloom, rim light, fog, embers, depth blur. He prefers heightened beauty to strict realism.
- **Colour:** Deep night blues and blacks against vivid effect colours: orange flame, cyan water, violet lightning, magic gold.
- **FX:** Hand-drawn elemental effects styled like woodblock prints (water as outlined waves with white foam curls, flame as brushed ribbons), combined with digital particles, sparks and 3DCG magic.
- **Sound effects:** Blade draw and ring, a breath before the strike, rushing water, fire roar, thunder crack, footsteps on wood and snow, wind in trees.
- **Prompt translation:**
  - "2D anime characters composited into a detailed 3D environment with matched lighting"
  - "the camera flies forward through the space in a true 3D move while the character stays hand-drawn"
  - "stylised water effect drawn like a traditional woodblock print, bold outlines and white foam curls"
  - "strong bloom, glowing embers and particles, rim light, depth-of-field blur"
  - "deep night-blue palette with vivid orange and cyan effect colours"
- **Avoid:** Flat uncomposited looks. Daylight flatness. Rough sketchy lines. Minimal effects.
- **Best for / worst for:** Best for supernatural sword fights, magic, night battles. Worst for sketchy, hand-made, low-compositing looks.
