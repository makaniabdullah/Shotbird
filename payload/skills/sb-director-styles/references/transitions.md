# Transitions

How one shot hands over to the next. A transition is written in **both** prompts it joins (the outgoing shot's last beat and the incoming shot's first frame), or inside one multi-shot prompt as a labelled line between shots. Always give: type, when it starts, how long it lasts, and what carries across (shape, motion direction, colour, sound).

In a single Seedance 2.5 generation, name each shot and the join between them: `SHOT 1 (0.0–4.0s) … TRANSITION at 4.0s: <type, duration, what carries> … SHOT 2 (4.0–8.0s) …`. One transition per join. A shot shorter than 3 s rarely holds.

Sound is part of every transition and is always SFX, never music: a whoosh, a door slam, a breath, a train, room tone changing.

## Cuts

| Term | What it is | Write |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut / straight cut | Instant change | "hard cut at 4.0s, no blend" |
| Match cut (graphic) | Same shape and screen position in both shots | "match cut: the round coffee cup, centred, becomes the full moon, same size and position" |
| Match on action | A movement starts in A and finishes in B | "she starts to stand in shot 1; shot 2 picks up the same rise from a wide, same speed" |
| Eyeline match | A looks off-frame, B shows what they see | "he looks frame right; cut to what he sees from his eye height" |
| Jump cut | Same framing, time skipped | "jump cut: same locked frame, she is now two metres closer, a different pose" |
| Smash cut | Abrupt cut to a strong contrast in loudness or energy | "smash cut from the silent held close-up to the roaring street; the sound slams in on the cut" |
| Cutaway / insert | Brief cut to a detail, then back | "0.8 s insert of the hand on the key, then back to the wide" |
| Cross-cut / parallel | Alternating between two actions | Name both locations and the rhythm: "alternate every 2 s" |
| Axial cut | Cut straight in or out along the lens axis | "cut in on the same axis from wide to medium to close, no angle change" |
| Cut on movement / whip cut | Cut hidden in fast motion | "cut during the fastest part of the head turn" |
| J-cut | Sound of B starts before the picture cuts | "the train's rumble begins 1 s before the cut to the platform" |
| L-cut | Sound of A carries over B | "her footsteps continue for 1 s over the next shot" |
| Invisible cut / hidden cut | A join disguised to look like one take | "camera passes behind the pillar, the frame goes fully dark for 4 frames, and continues in the next space" |
| Cut to black / cut from black | Hard to or from black | "cut to black at 5.0s; hold black 0.5 s" |
| Freeze-frame | Picture stops on a frame | "motion freezes at 4.5s and holds as a still for 1 s" |

## Camera-made transitions (done in the move, no post effect)

| Term | Write |
|---|---|
| Whip-pan transition | "shot 1 ends in a hard whip pan to the right, full motion-blur streak; shot 2 begins inside the same right-moving streak and lands locked" (same direction and speed on both sides) |
| Tilt-up / tilt-down to sky or floor | "tilts up into blank sky; shot 2 tilts down from blank sky into the new place" |
| Pass-through / wipe by object | "a passer-by crosses close to the lens and fills the frame; when they clear, we are in shot 2" |
| Body wipe / into the back | "camera pushes into the dark of his jacket until black; pulls out of a dark surface in shot 2" |
| Push through (window, keyhole, screen, eye) | "camera pushes through the window pane into the room beyond, one continuous move" |
| Zoom-through / crash zoom join | "crash zoom into the eye; the black pupil fills the frame; zoom out of a black tunnel mouth" |
| Rack-focus transition | "focus racks fully soft; shot 2 racks in from the same softness" |
| Spin / barrel-roll transition | "camera rolls 180° clockwise through the cut; shot 2 completes the roll" |
| Drop / rise through floor | "camera sinks through the floor line; shot 2 descends from the ceiling of the level below" |
| Orbit hand-off | "orbit passes behind her head, which blocks the lens; shot 2 comes out from behind a different head, same orbit direction" |
| Speed-ramp join | "ramps to very fast in the last 0.5 s of shot 1; shot 2 starts very fast and ramps down to real time" |
| Light flash / flare-out | "a headlight sweeps the lens and whites out the frame for 3 frames; shot 2 fades in from the white" |
| Shadow / darkness wipe | "a shadow crosses and takes the frame to black from left to right" |
| Drone-to-ground / FPV dive | "aerial dives down to street level and levels out at eye height without a cut" |
| Reflection or screen entry | "push into the reflection in the window until it fills the frame and becomes the next scene" |
| Dolly zoom as a turn | Use as a beat inside a shot, not across a cut |

## Optical and editorial transitions

| Term | Write |
|---|---|
| Dissolve / cross-dissolve | "1 s cross-dissolve; both images visible together at the midpoint" |
| Match dissolve | A dissolve between two matched shapes |
| Fade in / fade out | "fades from black over 1 s" / "fades to black over 1.5 s" |
| Dip to black / dip to white | "dips to black for 0.3 s between shots" |
| Wipe (direction) | "a hard vertical edge wipes left to right over 0.5 s" |
| Iris in / out | "a circle closes on her face to black" |
| Luma fade / burn | "highlights bloom to white first, then the whole frame" |
| Light leak / film burn | "orange light leak washes across for 8 frames" |
| Glitch / datamosh | "2–4 frames of blocky digital tearing, then the new shot" |
| Morph | "her face transforms smoothly into the older face over 1.5 s, features staying aligned" (high risk: keep framing and light identical on both sides) |
| Split screen | "frame divided vertically in two equal panels", say what is in each |
| Time-lapse bridge | "clouds and shadows race for 1 s; light changes from noon to dusk" |
| Reverse | "the action plays backwards from 3.0s" |
| Whiteout / blackout by weather | Smoke, fog, dust or rain thickens until the frame is featureless, then clears on the next place |

## Anime transitions

| Term | Write |
|---|---|
| Impact-frame cut | "one or two frames of inverted high-contrast black-and-white drawing on the hit, then cut to the reverse angle" |
| Smear transition | "the character's motion stretches into a drawn smear across the frame; the smear resolves into the next pose or shot" |
| Speed-line wipe | "radial or horizontal speed lines fill the frame for 4 frames and clear on the next shot" |
| White flash / black flash | "a single white frame on the cut" |
| Pan-cel hand-off | "the background slides fast in one direction until it is a streak, and stops on the new background" |
| Eyecatch | A short held graphic card between scenes (only if he asks; no text unless he gives it) |
| Postcard hold | "the final pose freezes into a painted, textured still and holds for 1.5 s" |
| Match cut on a drawn shape | Same as a graphic match cut, with identical line weight on both sides |
| Petal, feather or leaf wipe | "a cloud of drawn petals crosses the frame and clears on the new scene" |
| Iris and wipe shapes | Star, circle or diagonal wipes, for a retro look |

## Choosing when he only names the feeling

| He says | Use |
|---|---|
| Seamless / one take | Invisible cut, pass-through wipe, whip-pan transition, push through |
| Energetic | Whip pan, crash zoom join, speed-ramp join, smash cut |
| Dreamy / memory | Slow cross-dissolve, rack-focus transition, light leak, luma fade |
| Elegant / premium | Match cut, match on action, slow dip to black |
| Shock | Smash cut, impact-frame cut, cut to black with the sound cutting out |
| Passage of time | Dissolve, time-lapse bridge, fade out and in, jump cuts |
| Clever / witty | Graphic match cut, whip pan to a reaction, hard L-cut |

## What breaks
- A transition written in only one of the two prompts.
- Different direction or speed on the two sides of a whip, spin or speed-ramp join.
- Light, framing or subject scale that differ across a match cut or morph.
- More than one transition type on a single join.
- A music sting on the cut. Use a hard sound effect.
